Overview

Roam helps travellers plan a trip and keep using it on a plane, in a tunnel or abroad without roaming charges.

The challenge

Itineraries are edited on several devices, often offline, so naive syncing kept overwriting people’s changes.

The solution

Every change is stored in SQLite as an operation and replayed on reconnect. A small Swift module handles background sync and map tiles on iOS.

Results

  • Featured in the App Store’s travel category.
  • Every screen works fully offline.
  • The design system I built was reused in two later apps.
