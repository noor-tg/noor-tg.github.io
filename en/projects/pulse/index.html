Overview

Lumen’s customers wanted to see what their users were doing the moment it happened. Pulse is the pipeline and dashboard that makes that possible.

The challenge

Nightly batch reports were hours behind, and the old queue dropped events whenever traffic spiked.

The solution

Ingestion moved to a Go service that writes to ClickHouse in micro-batches. The Vue dashboard streams updates over WebSockets, so charts move as events arrive.

Results

  • Reports went from hours late to under a second.
  • Zero dropped events since launch.
  • Infrastructure cost fell by a third after the move.
