My story

I started in 2016 building websites for local businesses and learned that a finished thing beats a perfect thing. A love of mobile apps led me to Roam, and a love of reliable systems led me into DevOps.

Today I run Studio Altegany and work with early-stage founders. I like simple tools, small teams and frequent releases. I work from Linux in Alexandria with clients around the world.
