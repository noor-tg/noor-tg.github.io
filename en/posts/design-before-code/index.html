Why I sketch before I code

Ten minutes of sketching saves days of rewriting. My checklist for developers.

Developers often skip design because it feels like someone else’s job. When you work alone, it is yours, and a rough sketch is the cheapest way to find out you are building the wrong thing.

The four questions

Before opening the editor I answer four things on paper: who is this for, what is the one thing they must be able to do, what does the empty state look like and what happens when it fails. Most bugs I used to ship were answers to questions I had not asked.

Then show someone

Put the sketch in front of one real user for five minutes and stay quiet. What they hesitate over is your next task.
